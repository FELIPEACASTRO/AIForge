The exact 4.0 release date was not confirmed on the page. |
| [ARC-AGI-3 + ARC Prize 2026 ($2M)](https://arcprize.org/) | 2026 | Per arcprize.org: ARC-AGI-3 — described by ARC Prize as "the world's only unbeaten benchmark that measures agentic intelligence" — anchors the ARC Prize 2026 competition (reported $2,000,000 in prizes, run on Kaggle with ARC-AGI-2 and ARC-AGI-3 tracks). Still the current frontier-reasoning target in 2026; ARC-AGI-3 first previewed in 2025. |
| [Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI)](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks) | 2026 | Per epoch.ai/benchmarks: a composite capabilities index Epoch AI maintains across many benchmarks (GPQA Diamond, MATH Level 5, FrontierMath, SWE-bench Verified and more), tracking ~89 benchmarks in total as of the Oct 9 hub update. Exact ECI launch date not confirmed on the page. |
